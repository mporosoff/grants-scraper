      AI service

      Hosted AI sends the question, selected public institution, visible filters, bounded answer intent, and a bounded set of returned public award fields or abstract excerpts to Funding Finder's protected Cloudflare service. It never sends profiles, CVs, ORCID publication text, uploaded documents, saved notes, pursuit state, alert data, unrelated chat, or provider keys. Validated award records, not model pretraining, remain authoritative.

      Source notice: Award information comes from public NSF Award Search, NIH RePORTER, DOE Office of Science PAMS, and USAspending DoD prime assistance records. DoD coverage is limited to Project Grants (04) and Cooperative Agreements (05); contracts, IDVs, SBIR-specific feeds, and subawards are excluded. Missing values are shown as not listed or unavailable at the source; contact details are never inferred.

      Funding Finder is not an official source of record. Verify material details on the linked official award page.
