## Evidence and dates

Each approval binds the exact current SAM notice and organization IDs, complete
solicitation number, description hash and attachment-manifest hash. Official
solicitations and amendments establish academic eligibility and the actual open
entry route. Evidence includes explicit academic clauses and, where documented,
combined unrestricted eligibility and educational-offeror provisions. The latter
are identified as inferences rather than represented as verbatim universal clauses.

Required white papers, abstracts, concept papers and preproposals are represented
separately from subsequent invited full proposals. Optional preliminary dialogue
does not become a barrier. Annual cycles remain distinct; an invited-only tail
does not keep a closed entry cycle open. An anticipated Firefox full-proposal date
is explanatory text, not a firm deadline. Literal EST sources retain a fixed
offset with a qualification where the seasonal clock differs.

The PHMSA notice was amended on October 13. Its new current ID replaces the
superseded discovery ID. Several older standing calls have mismatched official
header and document dates. Where both still support a current route, the earlier
date bounds publication and the conflict is disclosed; no discrepancy is claimed
to be resolved. DLA Strategic Materials explicitly removed its initial cutoff
through an amendment, so that superseded date is not treated as closing the
continuing solicitation.

Classified or export-controlled branches are excluded from otherwise open calls
only where the official instructions support an independent unrestricted route.
EXPRESS explicitly makes DD2345 conditional and its expired restricted Call001
is excluded. Structural Materials remains withheld: its academic eligibility and
open date are verified, but the applicability of its DD2345 instruction needs
sponsor clarification. It is not labeled academically ineligible.

## Remaining limitations

NIWC `N6600126S4701` and NAWCWD `N6893626R0006` link to PIEE without sufficient
public eligibility/submission instructions. C5ISR `W56KGU23RRTID` exposes its
actual research topics only through a controlled attachment. Army Geospatial
`W5J9CQ25R0002` was open and academically eligible during review but closes at
14:00 Eastern on October 13; a protected release before that cutoff could not be
guaranteed. It is withheld for that operational reason, not deemed ineligible.

NIST CHIPS CRDO accepts academic applicants but had paused new applications on
September 15 according to its current official page. It is not advertised as
open. ARPA-H programs already represented by exact official URLs are not added
again through SAM. Research marketplaces that establish awardability without a
specific current funding call are recorded separately and not admitted as grants.

Approvals expire by November 12 unless renewed after review; daily metadata
collection does not silently extend eligibility approval. Changed source evidence
requires renewed review. Public metadata/manifest requests and source-document
research incurred no document-AI or embedding charges. The normal release's
existing paid limits, preserved caches and disabled team generation are unchanged.
