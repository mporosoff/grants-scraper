## 4. Repair the existing shared evidence handling

Use one shared implementation and a small explicit result contract separating:

1. Retrieval/ranking signals that make a candidate worth considering.
2. The contextual source-to-profile connections actually established by the matcher.
3. Whether those connections justify automatic membership or the group's scientific anchor under the stated exploratory standard.

These are internal distinctions, not new interface labels or claims of capability certification. Search can legitimately expose tentative leads without those leads automatically becoming team members. Preserve existing manual workflows and truthful uncertainty.

Reuse and extend the existing Search and Team Match evidence functions rather than copying formulas or writing another scientific interpreter. Both consumers must use the same evidence meaning. If they intentionally apply different discovery versus automatic-selection policies, name that difference explicitly instead of pretending their final lists must be identical.

### Preserve source provenance and context

Keep actual source title/description/document/selected-child text distinct from generated facets and administrative metadata. Derived facets can retrieve, explain a broad lead, or influence ordering; they cannot independently prove that the source asks for a scientific contribution. Preserve source field and location for the decisive match. Do not combine unrelated sentences, siblings, exclusions, or administrative sections into a fabricated scientific connection.

Use existing concept expansion, contextual acronym resolution and source evidence checks. A legitimate method transfer need not share the researcher's entire usual application or exact wording. Conversely, one generic term such as modeling, sensing, plasma or intelligence does not establish applicability by itself. Do not solve this with a growing blacklist or same-department requirement.

### Treat claims as claims, not a bag of duplicate strings

Preserve the exact audited summaries, labels and evidence. A claim label and its explanatory sentence are two representations of one claim, not two independent confirmations. Keep stable claim identity when consolidating evidence. Repetition, synonyms, or formatting alone must not increase support.

Reuse existing structured concept groups and relevant source clauses so a supported relationship is not lost merely because the audited evidence is a longer sentence. This is deterministic representation of existing information, not permission to invent shortened expertise claims, hand-author profiles for the benchmark, or add an LLM extractor.

Multiple partial hits may influence retrieval ranking. They must not be relabeled as a complete contextual match solely because their numerical sum crosses the old strong threshold. At the same time, do not require literal full-sentence overlap or reject every transfer whose words differ. Use the existing context-aware facilities and make remaining uncertainty explicit.

### Make the scientific anchor and explanations truthful

Do not use an aggregate `strong` number as a substitute for the evidence that connects a member to the source's scientific purpose. Return the actual supporting connections. An anchor based only on sponsor scope, tags or generic quality language is not a scientific anchor.

Retain exploratory relevance, not certified expertise. Accurate quotation/paraphrase attribution alone does not justify an assertion that the person supplies a particular capability. Keep the existing rendering contract but populate explanations only with the supported suggested connection and its retained evidence. Missing applicability remains unconfirmed.

Scientific corrections are allowed to change matching decisions. Do not demand byte-identical outputs to the faulty baseline. Record intentional differences and preserve unchanged behavior elsewhere; never update expected scientific results simply to hide an unexplained regression.
