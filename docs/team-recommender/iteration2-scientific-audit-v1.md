# Iteration 2 scientific interpretation audit

The accepted independent result remains 20 plausible and four insufficient-information verdicts; coverage remains 12 method-transfer, eight adjacent, four insufficient-information and zero direct. It differs from the saved Luna assessment on 19 role-coverage labels and ten derived person outcomes. These are observations, not a vote on which model is correct.

The audit read the complete retained source and conditions, interpreted contributions, all twelve full audited profiles and active claims, the assessment/checker contracts, outcome derivation, production verification and composer admission rules. Detailed per-person explanations remain in the local Iteration 1 report. The public handoff contains only this bounded methodological account.

The ECLIPSE source calls for fundamental plasma research with translational relevance. Its example directions are expressly nonexclusive. Cross-program challenges and societal/technological benefits are requirements for the proposed project narrative; a person's biography need not already articulate that future narrative to evidence a scientific contribution. Some retained rationales acknowledge a relevant documented activity but downgrade it because that narrative is absent. This is a possible rubric/interpretation ambiguity that warrants a general, versioned clarification for new evaluations, with both positive and negative fixtures. It does not justify manually relabeling the original answers.

The source also excludes technology-development projects primarily supported by other agencies. That actual scope restriction must remain visible for fusion-related applications. Fundamental plasma expertise is neither automatic project eligibility nor automatic exclusion merely because its previous application involved fusion. Distinguish documented contribution, a credible transferable-method bridge, future narrative obligations, a worthwhile conversation and supported team admission.

Two subject-boundary checks illustrate why the clarification must not become a general upgrade. Retained electrocatalysis evidence documents aqueous PFAS treatment, electrochemical interfaces, selective oxidation/CO2 reduction and pulsed-laser nanomaterial synthesis. The source's inclusion of plasmons and nanophotonics does not by itself establish that these retained claims document a plasma contribution. This remains an evidence limitation or unresolved bridge, not a demonstrated incorrect exclusion. Retained liquid-metal evidence documents electromagnetic conducting-fluid stability and industrial batteries/aluminum cells; it does not establish ionized-gas plasma expertise. A useful application alone cannot supply the missing scientific connection.

Four insufficient-information answers concern the translational contribution. Where the reason merely demands an already-written societal narrative, the same rubric ambiguity applies; where no documented method/application bridge exists, evidence remains insufficient. Neither distinction certifies anyone's general competence or incapacity. The original complete source/question/input and output identities remain unchanged. New selected-approach questions and clarified prompts receive new contract and evaluation identities.

General correction for new work: judge direct support against the documented activity needed for the specified contribution in this source context; do not require the biography to contain the future proposal narrative. Require an evidenced bridge for method transfer, preserve scope restrictions and uncertainty, and keep plausible conversation separate from supported graph admission. Optional scientific alternatives are considered directions, not simultaneous mandatory missing skills. The selected approach must be chosen from source evidence before candidate names.
