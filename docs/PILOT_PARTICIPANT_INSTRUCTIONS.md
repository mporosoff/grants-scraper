## Why we're doing this

Funding Finder searches current federal (and a growing set of other) funding
opportunities and can optionally use AI to re-rank and explain the best matches
for your work. Before we rely on it, we need to know two things from real
researchers:

1. **Does the search actually find the opportunities that are relevant to you?**
2. **When AI is used, does it put the genuinely useful ones near the top?**

Your ratings tell us where the search and ranking succeed or fail so we can fix
them. This is a usability and relevance evaluation of the tool — **not** a test
of you, and not a review of your research.

## What we'll ask you to do

1. **Open the tool** at the link above.
2. **Describe your work** in the search box (a topic, method, population, goal,
   or a specific program/opportunity number).
3. **Optionally add context** (recommended, because it makes the results more
   realistic): a short research description and expertise keywords, your
   applicant type and career stage, and — if you wish — upload your CV. *(Your
   CV is read inside your own browser to pull out text; the file itself is never
   uploaded or saved by us.)*
4. **Select "Find funding."** Review the results.
5. **Rate the opportunities you review.** Rating controls appear directly on
   each result card. For each one, answer *"How well does
   this match your work?"* — **Not a fit / Somewhat / Good fit / Strong fit /
   Can't tell** — and, when it isn't a good fit, pick a short reason (wrong
   topic, eligibility, career stage, deadline, award size, too much application
   burden, already known to you, or not enough detail to judge).
6. **Test personalization.** After three graded ratings, turn on
   **“Personalize from my ratings.”** Relevance sorting should update using only
   the ratings stored in your browser.
7. **Test the AI step (optional but valuable).** In the optional AI section,
   choose the provider shown in the box above, paste the test key, then select
   "Refine these results with AI" and rate the AI's top matches. Then use the
   **"Review AI candidates"** toggle and rate that pre-AI list too. *(This lets
   us tell "search missed it" apart from "AI ranked it poorly.")*
8. **Tell us what's missing.** If you know of an opportunity that is relevant to
   you but did **not** appear, note it in the "Overall note" box or tell the
   facilitator. Misses are some of the most valuable feedback.
9. **(Optional) Save a few** with the "☆ Save" button and check that the "★ Saved
   opportunities" list works for you.
10. **Send your feedback.** Use **"Export match ratings"** (and optionally "Send
    feedback") and return the file to the project facilitator through the agreed
    channel. You can also jot a few words on what was confusing or slow.

There are no right or wrong ratings — rate based on your honest judgment for
your own work.

## What information we collect

From the file you export:

- Your **ratings** and the **reason** you chose for each opportunity you review.
- The **current search text** used for that evaluation session. Search text may
  contain a research description, so use only non-confidential wording that you
  are comfortable returning to the project team.
- Which opportunities were shown, and **where they ranked** (in the search
  results and, if used, in the AI list).
- Whether AI was used, and which **AI provider/model** and prompt version.
- The **catalog date** and the **filters** you had applied.
- A short, non-identifying "profile fingerprint" (a code, not your text) so we
  can group ratings from the same session.
- If you complete the source-accuracy check: whether a cited fact matched the
  official notice, and your optional short note.

## What we do NOT collect

- **The AI key** (it stays in your browser and goes only to the AI provider —
  never to us).
- **Your CV** or its text.
- **Your saved profile fields, expertise field, and CV text.** The match-ratings
  export does not include these fields; only the separately entered search-box
  text is included.
- **Any AI chat** you have with the results.

Your ratings are stored **only in your browser** until you choose to export and
send them. Nothing is transmitted automatically.

If you explicitly run AI refinement or chat, the selected AI provider receives
the current search question, bounded public opportunity records, and any
profile context or CV excerpt you left enabled. The provider's billing,
retention, and privacy terms apply. Funding Finder and the project team do not
receive the API key or AI conversation.

## How we'll use it

We aggregate everyone's ratings to measure how often the search *retrieves* the
relevant opportunities and how well the AI *ranks* them, and to identify the top
failure modes (e.g., wrong eligibility, stale deadlines, missing sources). We
use that to tune the search, ranking, and data sources. Results are reported in
aggregate; **no individual participant is identified.** Returned files are kept
privately by the project team and are **not** posted to the public site.

## Please keep in mind

- **Do not enter confidential or unpublished research** into the tool or the
  notes. Use general descriptions of your interests.
- **Always verify details on the official notice** before acting — the tool can
  be wrong, and some facts are machine-extracted and marked "verify."
- **Participation is voluntary.** You may skip anything or stop at any time.
- If something breaks or looks wrong, that's useful — please note it.

## Taking part & contact

By completing and returning your ratings, you're agreeing to help with this
tool evaluation as described above. This is an internal evaluation of the
software, not a study of you or your research.

Questions, problems, or to return your feedback file:
contact the project facilitator through the agreed channel.
