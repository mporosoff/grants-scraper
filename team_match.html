    Matching opportunities

    Team Match screens current parent opportunities and publication-eligible child topics using the same local fielded and optional hosted semantic search assets as Funding Finder. Every displayed opportunity must also pass the team-specific evidence check for every selected researcher. Archived calls are excluded. Browser-only researcher profiles are stored only on this device.

    Enhanced ordering may send a bounded aggregate of selected research keywords and theme labels. Researcher names and publication text are not sent. The hosted ranking can only reorder opportunities that already passed every-researcher local fit.

    Use notice: Team Match is an informational research-planning aid. It is not an official source of record, application or submission system, or legal, financial, compliance, or sponsor guidance.

    Opportunity and researcher-match information may be incomplete, outdated, or machine-generated. Before acting or applying, independently verify fit, eligibility, deadlines, requirements, and terms in the sponsor's official notice.
