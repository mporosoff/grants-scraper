Expand and refine your search with AI

Connect an AI provider Provider, key, cost, Help, and privacy details Not configured

What your connected AI changes: after catalog search, it can use the profile context you enabled, try alternative scientific phrases, assess up to 32 locally qualified catalog candidates, and answer questions about results or an uploaded notice. Refinement only adds evidence-qualified Strong matches; it never removes ordinary results or searches outside the funding catalog.

You do not need a key for normal search. AI refinement requires your own provider key. Create a separate, limited-use key and review its spending controls before saving it here: OpenAI key and project limits or Anthropic key safety and limits.

A saved key stays in this browser's local storage until you remove it. Anyone using this browser profile could use it, so do not save a key on a shared device. Research text and an enabled CV excerpt go directly to the selected provider only when you choose AI refinement or chat.

Your connected AI uses two bounded calls for refinement: alternative scientific phrases, then assessment of at most 32 locally qualified candidates. Local Strong matching does not use a model; hosted Potential matching uses the site's service and key.

Run a funding search and enter or save an AI provider key to enable refinement.
