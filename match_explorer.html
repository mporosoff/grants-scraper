AI service and privacy Hosted AI is included; personal provider keys remain optional Not configured

No API key is required. Funding Finder routes each feature through its tested hosted model.

Hosted AI sends only the bounded information needed for the feature to Funding Finder's protected Cloudflare service; provider keys are never sent to the browser. If you choose your own provider, a saved key stays in this browser's local storage until you remove it. Advanced users may instead select OpenAI or Anthropic and provide their own limited-use key after reviewing its spending controls: OpenAI key and project limits or Anthropic key safety and limits.
